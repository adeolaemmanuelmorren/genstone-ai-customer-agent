## Website Conflicts

### WEB-C001 - Fireplace hand test

- One FAQ says the installation surface should remain touchable for five seconds while the appliance is running. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/can-genstone-be-used-around-fireplaces)
- Another FAQ says ten seconds. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/is-genstone-fire-rated)
- A design page gives no number and says only that the surface must not be too hot to touch. [Source](https://genstone.com/insights/design-ideas/faux-stone-fireplace)
- **Owner question:** What exact, approved fireplace heat and clearance language may callers receive?

### WEB-C002 - Big Stretch caulk quantity

- A heading says one unit for every 400 square feet.
- The body says one tube for every 100 square feet and at least one tube for smaller projects.
- Both statements appear on the same page. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)
- **Owner question:** What is the current quantity rule, and does it differ by panel system or application?

### WEB-C003 - Shaffer Fireplace Kit width

- The current product page says 60 inches wide by 108 inches tall. [Source](https://genstone.com/products/fireplace-kits/shaffer-fireplace-kit)
- A current design article says 65 inches wide by 108 inches tall. [Source](https://genstone.com/insights/design-ideas/fireplace-surround-kits)
- **Owner question:** What is the finished nominal width of the current Shaffer kit?

### WEB-C004 - Pillar post size

- The system page says pillar panels fit a square post of 7.75 inches or less. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/the-genstone-system)
- Another installation page describes the system as designed for an 8-inch post. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/5-common-questions-solutions)
- **Owner question:** Is “8-inch post” only nominal wording, with 7.75 inches as the maximum actual size?

### WEB-C005 - Chicago and Deep Red color descriptions

- Current Chicago and Deep Red product pages both say “Rich Burgundy with Crimson Undertones.” [Chicago source](https://genstone.com/products/brick/chicago) [Deep Red source](https://genstone.com/products/brick/deep-red)
- **Owner question:** Are both descriptions intentional, or is one product page mislabeled?

### WEB-C006 - Delivery language

- The formal policy gives no guarantee and uses weight, stock, warehouse, and carrier conditions. [Source](https://genstone.com/shipping-policy)
- Comparison pages make broader “ready to ship” or three-to-five-day statements. [Source](https://genstone.com/comparison-hub/genstone-vs-airstone)
- **Owner question:** Should all caller-facing delivery estimates be limited to the formal policy wording?

### WEB-C007 - Product waterproof percentage and flashing

- A current FAQ says correctly installed panels are 100% waterproof and require no flashing. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/are-genstone-panels-waterproof)
- Older technical articles say the material is 99.9% waterproof. [Source](https://genstone.com/insights/advice-and-insights/top-5-questions-and-qualities)
- A chimney FAQ says no flashing, while detailed installation tips require flashing at openings and above ledgers. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/how-do-you-install-genstone-panels-on-a-chimney) [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- **Owner question:** What waterproof and flashing language accurately distinguishes panel material, panel joints, openings, ledgers, and the complete wall assembly?

### WEB-C008 - Direct installation versus framework

- Product pages say GenStone fastens to existing flat surfaces including wood, brick, stucco, concrete, and drywall. [Source](https://genstone.com/products/brick)
- An insulation article says panels are installed on a framework just in front of the building rather than affixed to it. [Source](https://genstone.com/insights/comparisons/insulation-r-values)
- **Owner question:** Is the framework language outdated or limited to a particular exterior assembly?

### WEB-C009 - Painting finished panels

- A general FAQ says faux stone siding can often be painted with proper preparation. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/can-you-paint-faux-stone-siding)
- The current warranty excludes product painted or surface-altered without written authorization. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- **Owner question:** May customers repaint finished GenStone, and what written approval or product-specific process is required?

### WEB-C010 - Proposition 65 wording

- The current warning identifies exposure to a California-listed carcinogen. [Source](https://genstone.com/california-prop-65-warning)
- An older sustainability article broadly describes the production materials as inert, non-toxic, and compliant with Proposition 65. [Source](https://genstone.com/insights/advice-and-insights/top-5-questions-and-qualities)
- **Owner question:** What approved safety wording should accompany the required warning?

### WEB-C011 - Warranty scope by product family

- The warranty landing page says all interior and exterior GenStone products receive the 25-year warranty. [Source](https://genstone.com/warranty)
- The current warranty document defines faux stone, rock, and brick products and does not clearly include faux wood or electric fireplaces. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- **Owner question:** What warranties apply separately to stone/brick, faux wood, electric fireplaces, kits, caulk, paint, and other accessories?

### WEB-C012 - Visualizer service and price

- One page describes an instant, no-app, AI self-service visualizer covering 19 choices. [Source](https://genstone.com/see-genstone-on-your-home)
- Another page presents a photo-submission rendering form with a crossed-out $50 fee and a free offer. [Source](https://genstone.com/visualizer)
- **Owner question:** Are these distinct services, and which service and price may be described as currently available?

## Transcript Conflicts and Open Questions

> Transcript evidence is deidentified in [`raw/transcripts/transcript-findings.md`](raw/transcripts/transcript-findings.md). Caller and answering-agent statements are not treated as policy.

### CALL-C001 - Included paint, caulk, adhesive, and tutorials

Multiple callers said instructions or sales materials led them to expect touch-up paint, color-matched caulk, adhesive, or a tutorial with panel orders, while other customers knowingly ordered these as separate accessories. Confirm inclusion rules at SKU, kit, and sales-channel level and correct any instructions that imply a universal bundle.

### CALL-C002 - Price and unit of sale

Callers repeatedly could not tell whether prices represented one panel, a box, a post side, a four-sided post kit, or a cap-inclusive bundle. Confirm the required unit-of-sale and package-content fields for every product and channel.

### CALL-C003 - Installation service and service area

Callers routinely assumed GenStone sends installers or local project coordinators. Confirm whether GenStone offers installation, referrals, territory representatives, or remote consultation only, and what geographic claims are approved.

### CALL-C004 - Store, showroom, display, and stock

Callers were often told only about the Colorado office even when asking where they could see or buy product locally. Confirm which locations are showrooms, stocked displays, sample-only displays, special-order retailers, distributors, or corporate offices.

### CALL-C005 - Processing time versus transit time

Callers interpreted “ground,” “two days,” “three to five days,” and sale-related statements as delivery promises. Confirm approved definitions for order processing, stock allocation, carrier transit, freight appointment, sample delivery, backorder, and expedited service.

### CALL-Q001 - Visualizer services

Confirm upload acknowledgment, supported file requirements, automated versus human rendering, free versus paid/custom service, turnaround, status visibility, and recovery when a submission fails.

### CALL-Q002 - Damage and shortage workflow

Confirm where evidence is submitted, what photos/labels/packaging are required, whether material must be retained, how quickly receipt is acknowledged, and how customers see claim/replacement status.

### CALL-Q003 - Wrong item/color and split shipment

Define the approved response when some boxes are refused, some installed, some missing, or a replacement does not match the original SKU/color. Include item-level tracking and case-history requirements.

### CALL-Q004 - Order change, cancellation, and replacement hold

Confirm the operational cutoff for changes/cancellation, how a pending replacement can be held, and what acknowledgment tells the customer whether warehouse release was actually stopped.

### CALL-Q005 - Color matching older material

Define the approved process and caveat for matching products purchased years earlier, including profile/color identification, lot variation, weathering, samples, and availability of compatible paint/caulk.

### CALL-Q006 - Fingered corners as standalone accents

Callers repeatedly wanted to run fingered corners vertically without adjacent wall panels. Confirm whether this application is approved and, if so, the exact filler/return treatment and component diagram.

### CALL-Q007 - Terminal cut edges

Confirm approved treatments where a panel run ends at a fireplace opening, door/window, straight wall edge, or transition to conventional siding. Identify when trim, J-channel, paint, caulk, or another assembly is appropriate.

### CALL-Q008 - Paint kit directions and allowed uses

Confirm each bottle's contents, mixing/sequence, application technique, safety/cleanup, compatible GenStone products, storage life, and whether non-GenStone substrates or decorative objects are supported.

### CALL-Q009 - Professional and partner programs

Confirm current programs and contacts for contractors, designers, national accounts, wholesalers, retail branches, samples/literature, distributor lookup, pricing, inventory, PO status, RGA, and return disposition.

### CALL-Q010 - Shipment contact data

Investigate why multiple carriers received the GenStone number instead of the recipient's phone number and why addresses sometimes lost unit or correction data. Confirm the source-of-truth fields and validation before label creation.

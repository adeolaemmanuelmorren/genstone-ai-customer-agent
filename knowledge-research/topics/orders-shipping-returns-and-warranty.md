# Orders, Shipping, Returns, Claims, and Warranty

> Draft pending business-owner approval. Website facts are supplemented by deidentified transcript needs; Zendesk findings have not yet been merged.

## Delivery Expectations

- GenStone's shipping policy explicitly says delivery dates are not guaranteed. [Source](https://genstone.com/shipping-policy)
- For in-stock orders under 120 pounds, the policy says delivery throughout the contiguous United States can occur in three days or less when inventory is available from one warehouse or a combination of warehouses. Hawaii and Alaska are excluded from that statement. [Source](https://genstone.com/shipping-policy)
- For orders over 120 pounds, the policy says most deliveries occur in four days or less depending on carrier availability. This is not a guarantee. [Source](https://genstone.com/shipping-policy)
- A comparison page says GenStone is ready to ship in three to five days. Because that marketing statement differs from the formal policy's weight-based wording, use the shipping policy and avoid promising a date. [Source](https://genstone.com/comparison-hub/genstone-vs-airstone)

## Delivery Shortages or Damage

- After delivery, customers should open every box and inventory the contents. [Source](https://genstone.com/shipping-policy)
- The formal policy requires written notice of damaged or incomplete shipments within seven days of delivery. A shortage claim identifies the missing quantities; a damage claim includes photos of the damaged product and its packaging. [Source](https://genstone.com/shipping-policy)
- The public claim page says the support team will follow up after the claims form is submitted. Do not promise a resolution before review. [Source](https://genstone.com/claim-or-return)

## Returns

- Direct GenStone returns must be postmarked within 90 days of delivery and be new and unused. [Source](https://genstone.com/return-policy)
- The customer is responsible for return shipping. The policy recommends a trackable shipping method. [Source](https://genstone.com/return-policy)
- After receipt and inspection, the policy asks customers to allow at least 10 days for return processing. A card refund may then take one or two billing cycles to appear. [Source](https://genstone.com/return-policy)
- A color exchange after fulfillment is allowed for an order that remains new, including an order still in transit, but the customer pays the cost to return the original color and ship the replacement color. [Source](https://genstone.com/return-policy)
- If GenStone has already incurred fulfillment costs before a cancellation, the refund may include cancellation fees. [Source](https://genstone.com/return-policy)
- Installed or otherwise customer-damaged products cannot be returned under the normal return policy. Defective or transit-damaged products follow the claim process. [Source](https://genstone.com/return-policy)
- A full or mostly full original-order return is subject to a 25% restocking fee at GenStone's discretion. A partial return caused by ordering excess project material is not subject to that restocking fee. [Source](https://genstone.com/return-policy)
- Refund quantity is based on the warehouse's inspection of items that are in good condition and can be returned to inventory. [Source](https://genstone.com/return-policy)
- Retailer purchases must be returned through the retailer. [Source](https://genstone.com/claim-or-return)

## 25-Year Limited Warranty

- The current warranty document covers manufacturing defects in GenStone stone and brick products when they are installed according to GenStone's guide and maintained according to its care document. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- The current document describes a 25-year limited warranty from the original purchase date. It is limited to the original purchaser and is not transferable to a later property owner. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- If GenStone determines that a valid manufacturing defect exists, the document allows GenStone to choose an applicable remedy: repair materials such as paint, caulk, or sealant; replacement of the affected portion; or a prorated refund for the affected portion. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- Warranty obligations do not exceed the purchase price of the affected product portion. Additional costs and labor are not covered. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- Coverage is prorated: 100% in the first year, then declining through specified bands to 10% during years 20 through 25. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- The document excludes improper use or installation, structural movement, normal weathering or fading, failure to perform recommended care, stains or surface pollution, mold or mildew, extreme environmental expansion or contraction, exterior use of interior products, labor, shipping or storage damage, fire and excessive heat, disasters, unpainted raw polyurethane exposed to sunlight, impact, and unauthorized painting or surface alteration. This is a summary; the current warranty controls. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- A claim must be submitted in writing during the warranty period and within 30 days after the defect is or should have been discovered. GenStone may require photos, measurements, samples, proof of purchase, or other information to evaluate the claim. [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- The warranty claim form is an application for review, not evidence that a claim is covered or will be approved. [Source](https://genstone.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/GenStone-Warranty-Claim.pdf)

## Warranty Scope That Needs Confirmation

- The website landing page says the 25-year warranty comes with all interior and exterior GenStone products, but the current warranty PDF is titled for stone and brick and defines its covered products as faux stone, rock, and brick. Confirm whether faux wood and electric fireplaces have the same warranty. [Landing-page source](https://genstone.com/warranty) [Warranty source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- The warranty excludes unauthorized painting or surface alteration, while a general FAQ says faux stone siding can often be painted with proper preparation. Do not advise repainting a finished panel without written GenStone approval. [Warranty source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty) [Conflicting FAQ](https://genstone.com/faq/can-you-paint-faux-stone-siding)

## California Proposition 65

- The current warning page states that the product can expose users to toluene diisocyanate, which California lists as causing cancer. [Source](https://genstone.com/california-prop-65-warning)
- An older sustainability article says GenStone products comply with Proposition 65 and describes production materials as inert and non-toxic. Because that broad wording sits beside the current warning, callers should receive the current warning without an unsupported safety interpretation. [Source](https://genstone.com/insights/advice-and-insights/top-5-questions-and-qualities)

## Transcript-Driven Service Clarity

The calls identify recurring service gaps but do not supersede the formal policies above. [Transcript evidence](../raw/transcripts/transcript-findings.md)

- Confirmation should immediately identify the order number, items and quantities, unit/price basis, billing and shipping addresses, payment status, promotion, and next expected step.
- Order status should show each line item's fulfillment state, each package and tracking number, carrier, backorder, and delivery appointment where applicable.
- Shipping messages should distinguish processing from transit time, link directly to carrier tracking, and include the carrier's PRO/pickup identifiers and recipient contact information.
- The repeated carrier calls about GenStone's phone number or incomplete addresses require an operational label-data investigation; the knowledge base should not normalize that behavior.
- A claim submission should return a case number and evidence receipt, state whether product/packaging must be retained, and expose the next response or status.
- Cancellation, order change, return, exchange, replacement, and partial-overage return are different intents and should be confirmed separately.
- Return handling should support printable labels, verified alternate-email delivery, and carrier-supported non-print options. Retail partners need RGA, credit, freight, and disposition instructions.
- No claim, replacement, refund, cancellation, delivery, or promotion exception should be promised until the responsible team confirms it.

# Purchasing, Samples, Estimating, and Visualizer

> Draft pending business-owner approval. Website facts are supplemented by deidentified transcript needs; Zendesk findings have not yet been merged.

## Where to Buy

- GenStone is sold directly through GenStone's website and through major retailers including Home Depot, Lowe's, and Amazon. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/where-can-you-buy-genstone)
- The website also describes purchasing through other retailers or with assistance from a GenStone Project Coordinator. Availability and pricing can differ by seller. [Source](https://genstone.com/getting-started-guide)
- Returns for purchases made through a retailer must be initiated with that retailer. [Source](https://genstone.com/claim-or-return)
- The store-finder page currently says its search covers Canada, Alaska, and Hawaii. Because the FAQ describes broader retailer availability, do not characterize the store finder as a complete national inventory without owner confirmation. [Source](https://genstone.com/where-to-buy-genstone)

## Samples

- A 12-by-12-inch sample is intended for evaluating texture, depth, durability, weight, cutting, and fastening. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/sample-size-suggestions)
- A 12-by-12-inch stacked-stone sample may not contain the full range of highlights found across a complete panel. A full stacked-stone panel is the better website recommendation when the decision depends on the full color profile. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/sample-size-suggestions)
- Standard 12-by-12-inch brick samples generally show the hues and texture of the selected brick color, but the website recommends a half panel for Multi Color brick and for evaluating how brick panels interconnect. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/sample-size-suggestions)
- Color should be evaluated in the intended space, at different times of day, and against surrounding materials because sunlight and shadow can change the appearance. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/5-sample-testing-tips)

## Two Panels in Two Days Program

- The current promotional page advertises two full-size stone or brick panels with two-day delivery for a stated promotional price, followed by a credit for the selected color when the full project order is purchased. [Source](https://genstone.com/2p2d)
- The page says the selected panel can be kept and used in the project. It also says the unselected color may be kept or returned, but only the selected color receives the program refund. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/can-i-keep-my-full-panel-samples)
- A current FAQ says the sample credit request is submitted after the full order and is usually issued within one business day. Because this is a promotional and accounting promise, confirm the current terms before quoting timing or price. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/how-long-does-it-take-to-get-my-sample-credit)

## Estimating and Project Pricing

- The project calculator uses installation dimensions to estimate panels and accessories. [Source](https://genstone.com/price-my-project)
- A complete estimate may need panels, corners, trim or ledgers, finished edges, caulk, paint, adhesive, fasteners, substrate preparation, and a waste factor. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/how-much-do-faux-stone-panels-cost) [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/how-do-i-estimate-how-much-genstone-i-need-for-my-project)
- Website prices and promotional discounts are changeable. This draft does not preserve individual SKU prices or promise a percentage discount.

## Visualizer Options

- The self-service visualizer is described as phone-friendly and requires no app. A user uploads a photo, and the tool identifies wall areas while excluding features such as doors and windows. [Source](https://genstone.com/see-genstone-on-your-home)
- The self-service page says users can cycle through all 19 standard choices: nine stone colors, five brick colors, and five wood finishes. [Source](https://genstone.com/see-genstone-on-your-home)
- The page describes both interior and exterior use, including fireplaces, accent walls, and exterior siding areas. [Source](https://genstone.com/see-genstone-on-your-home)
- After visualizing a color, the website offers a path to project pricing and material selection. [Source](https://genstone.com/see-genstone-on-your-home)
- A separate visualizer page presents a photo-upload form for a custom rendering and shows a crossed-out fee with a current free offer. Treat that as a promotional offer, not a permanent pricing promise. [Source](https://genstone.com/visualizer)

## Project Assistance

- The public Project SOS service advertises help with color selection, visualizations, measurements or material takeoffs, and installation questions. [Source](https://genstone.com/project-sos)
- The website describes this assistance as available without an additional service fee, but product, delivery, or other purchase costs still apply. [Source](https://genstone.com/project-sos)

## Pro Program

- The Pro Program is described as a business program for builders, remodelers, contractors, fireplace or accent-wall installers, and design-build firms rather than homeowners. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/is-the-pro-program-for-homeowners) [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/who-is-the-pro-program-designed-for)
- Website-listed benefits include pro pricing, project renderings, quote support, and installation guidance. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/what-is-the-genstone-pro-program)
- Claims about margin, speed, or profitability are marketing estimates and should not be presented as guaranteed outcomes. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/how-does-the-pro-program-help-contractors-increase-margin)

## Transcript-Driven Purchasing Needs

Across the 428 calls, shoppers and partners repeatedly lacked information needed to choose and order safely. [Transcript evidence](../raw/transcripts/transcript-findings.md)

- A store finder should label each result as corporate office, showroom, stocked display, sample display, stocking dealer, special-order retailer, distributor, or order-only location.
- Product listings should state the eligible sales channel for each promotion and whether a code applies automatically, excludes products, or cannot be used on a marketplace/retailer order.
- The visualizer should acknowledge uploads, state whether the result is automated or human-produced, show status/turnaround, distinguish free and paid/custom services, and offer recovery after upload failure.
- Partner support needs a dedicated path for SKU pricing, live availability, samples, brochures, distributor lookup, territory representatives, project quotes, and large-quantity coverage checks.
- Samples should be routed to the named store department or attention line, and physical samples should be recommended where color matching matters.
- Assisted ordering should never imply that reception can access a cart or payment details. A trusted sales path should identify itself clearly and preserve documented promotions when the business approves an exception.

## What GenStone Is

- GenStone is a panelized faux stone and faux brick system intended to create a masonry appearance with a simpler, mortar-free installation. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/what-is-genstone)
- Stone and brick panels are molded from real stone or brick shapes. Website sources describe the stone panels as closed-cell, high-density polyurethane that is finished to reproduce natural texture and color. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/is-genstone-real-stone) [Source](https://genstone.com/insights/advice-and-insights/top-5-questions-and-qualities)
- The product system includes panels and purpose-built finishing pieces rather than only individual faux stones. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/the-genstone-system)
- Standard panels are intended for interior and exterior applications, subject to product-specific specifications, substrate requirements, heat clearances, and the installation guide. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/can-genstone-be-installed-indoors-and-outdoors)

## Component Terminology

- **Panel:** The main field piece used to cover a flat installation area. Stone, brick, and wood panels have different nominal dimensions and coverage. [Source](https://genstone.com/products)
- **Shiplap:** The overlapping panel edges that connect adjacent pieces. Website instructions refer to positive and negative shiplap edges and direct installers to keep connections tight. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- **Inside corner / outside corner:** A preformed 90-degree piece used to transition between walls without field-mitering standard panels. [Source](https://genstone.com/price-my-project)
- **Ledger:** A sloped finishing piece commonly used at the top of a wainscot. Straight ledgers list 42 inches, or 3.5 linear feet, of coverage; matching inside- and outside-corner ledgers continue the finish around corners. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/how-much-do-i-need)
- **Trim:** A 42-inch finishing piece used around obstacles or as a stopping point for a partial-wall installation. [Source](https://genstone.com/price-my-project)
- **Left/right finished edge or return:** A 12 by 12 inch stacked-stone piece that can finish a partial wall or carry stone around an edge as an alternative to trim or a painted cut edge. [Source](https://genstone.com/price-my-project)
- **Wall cap:** A matching stone-system cap used to finish the top of a wall. [Source](https://genstone.com/products/faux-stone-panels/arctic-smoke/arctic-smoke-wall-cap)
- **Paint kit:** Matching colors used to cover exposed raw material after cutting and to tint matching caulk. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)
- **Big Stretch caulk:** The elastic caulk recommended by the website for weather-tight connections between panels and finishing pieces. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)
- **Exact Color caulk:** Tintable caulk used to conceal exposed fastener holes and some cut areas. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)

## Installation Overview

- GenStone describes the basic process as **measure, cut, and attach**. The panels are intended for DIY installation with common woodworking tools rather than masonry tools. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/the-genstone-system)
- Common tools listed across the website include a circular saw or other wood-cutting saw, drill, level, fasteners appropriate for the substrate, adhesive where required, caulk, and eye and breathing protection for cutting. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/do-i-need-special-tools-to-install-genstone-panels) [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- Begin from a level reference line, keep shiplap connections tight, stagger vertical seams, and use matching accessories or approved finishing methods at corners, edges, penetrations, and transitions. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/how-do-you-install-faux-stone-panels) [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- Save usable offcuts; the website describes an average 5% scrap factor for estimating. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- Touch up exposed raw polyurethane after cutting because unpainted material can yellow in sunlight. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)

## Substrates

- Interior drywall can be used when it is sound and panels are fastened into studs or another secure mounting point. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/can-genstone-panels-be-installed-over-drywall)
- The detailed installation tips call for at least 7/16-inch wood sheathing with housewrap for a typical exterior wall. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- Concrete, cinder block, brick, and stucco installations generally use hardened masonry fasteners such as Tapcon-type fasteners plus the required adhesive pattern. The surface must be flat, sound, and dry. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/how-do-you-install-genstone-panels-on-a-foundation-concrete-brick-stucco) [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- Existing siding or uneven masonry may need a prepared substrate or furring to create a secure, level surface. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/can-you-put-faux-stone-over-siding) [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/can-you-put-faux-stone-panels-over-brick)
- For ribbed metal siding, the website describes creating a level surface with 1-by-2 furring strips secured with construction adhesive and self-tapping screws. Partial-height installations also use closure strips above the GenStone to close the rib gaps. [Source](https://genstone.com/insights/design-ideas/metal-building-siding)

## Fasteners and Adhesive

- A detailed website guide says a stacked-stone field panel on suitable sheathing uses 10 screws: four along the top shiplap, four face screws near the bottom, and two along the right shiplap. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- The same guide says a standard stone corner uses at least eight screws, a full brick panel uses eight screws, and a half brick panel uses four. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- For concrete or cinder block, that guide calls for at least eight masonry fasteners per panel plus a generous approved adhesive pattern. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)
- Construction adhesive is described as required for exterior installations and generally suggested for interior installations except over drywall. The reviewed page recommends an S-shaped pattern on the back of panels, ledgers, and trim. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)
- Fastener type and length must match the substrate and current installation guide. The exact guide for the selected product should control over this draft summary. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install)

## Caulk, Paint, and Finishing Quantities

- Big Stretch caulk is applied at panel shiplaps and at ledger, trim, and substrate connections to create weather-tight joints. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)
- The body of the current caulking page recommends one tube of Big Stretch for each 100 square feet, including at least one tube for smaller projects. A heading on the same page says “one every 400 SQFT,” so this quantity is unresolved and must be confirmed before publication. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)
- The page recommends one paint kit per 400 square feet for cut-edge touch-up and one paint kit per tube of Exact Color caulk used for tinting. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)
- It recommends one tube of Exact Color caulk for a small project or one tube per 400 square feet on larger projects. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/adhesive-caulking-and-paint)
- The detailed tips call for a 3/16-inch gap between cut GenStone edges and trim, windows, doors, steps, or similar terminations, followed by touch-up paint and matching caulk. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/tips-to-finish-like-a-pro)

## Measuring

- Measure the length and height of every installation area. For large areas, multiply length by height for square footage, add all walls, then divide by the listed coverage of the selected panel. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/how-much-do-i-need)
- For small areas measured in inches, divide the width by panel coverage width and the height by panel coverage height, then multiply the horizontal and vertical quantities. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/how-much-do-i-need)
- Subtract true voids such as doors and windows from the total installation area. Account separately for corners, trim, ledgers, and waste. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/how-much-do-i-need) [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/how-do-i-estimate-how-much-genstone-i-need-for-my-project)
- Straight trim and ledgers list 42 inches, or 3.5 linear feet, of coverage. Divide the required run by 42 inches or 3.5 feet, using consistent units. [Source](https://genstone.com/how-to-install/how-much-do-i-need)

- Panels and accessories must not contact flames or embers, and appliance manufacturer clearances and local requirements must be followed. [Source](https://genstone.com/faq/can-genstone-be-used-around-fireplaces) [Source](https://on.genstone.com/genstone_warranty)
- The website gives conflicting hand-on-surface tests of five seconds and ten seconds, while another page only says the surface must not be too hot to touch. Do not publish a numeric hand test until the owner confirms the approved rule. ## Cleaning and Preventive Care

- Normal rainfall is often sufficient. For moderate dirt, the official care guide recommends clear water from a garden hose and a soft-bristled brush, with special attention to sheltered areas under eaves, porches, awnings, and other overhangs. [Source](https://genstone.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/GenStone-Products-Use-and-Care.pdf)
- For heavier dirt, the guide lists a solution of 1/3 cup detergent or dish soap, 2/3 cup trisodium phosphate, and one gallon of water. Follow chemical manufacturer safety instructions. [Source](https://genstone.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/GenStone-Products-Use-and-Care.pdf)
- For mildew, the guide refers to its basic cleaning solution with sodium hypochlorite, but the reviewed PDF does not display a sodium-hypochlorite quantity. Do not invent a concentration. [Source](https://genstone.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/GenStone-Products-Use-and-Care.pdf)
- For caulk, tar, or similar substances, the guide allows reasonable amounts of mineral spirits applied directly to the substance, followed immediately by a thorough water rinse. Test any cleaner in an inconspicuous area first. [Source](https://genstone.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/GenStone-Products-Use-and-Care.pdf)
- Avoid abrasive cleaners, strong solvents, excessive scrubbing, and stronger-than-recommended cleaner concentrations. Always rinse with clear water to minimize streaking. [Source](https://genstone.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/GenStone-Products-Use-and-Care.pdf)
- Inspect GenStone at least yearly and touch up any exposed, unpainted raw material. The care guide also recommends a water-based concrete sealant every five years or at the first sign of fading. [Source](https://genstone.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/GenStone-Products-Use-and-Care.pdf)
- The care guide names one sealant and says a similar water-based concrete sealant may be used, but also calls for written approval before using another brand. Confirm the approved current sealant policy before naming alternatives. [Source](https://genstone.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/GenStone-Products-Use-and-Care.pdf)

## Transcript-Driven Installation Guardrails

The calls show recurring applications that need diagrams or explicit boundaries; caller and answering-agent statements are not treated as technical approval. [Transcript evidence](../raw/transcripts/transcript-findings.md)

- A fireplace intake should capture appliance type/model, manufacturer clearances, combustible/noncombustible zones, front opening, wall returns/depth, outside/inside corners, mantel/TV/hearth, substrate, and terminal-edge treatment before estimating.
- GenStone is decorative veneer, not a substitute for a listed wood-stove wall-protection system, structural retaining wall, drainage plane, waterproofing system, chimney flashing, or engineered substrate.
- Provide approved terminal details for cut edges at fireplace openings, doors, windows, steps, conventional siding, and freestanding vertical accents.
- Confirm whether fingered corners may be installed as standalone columns/accents and, if allowed, show the approved return/filler treatment.
- Estimates should expose net area, opening deductions, per-panel coverage, whole-piece corner counts, course height, accessory quantities, and waste/layout assumptions. Validate units and arithmetic before ordering.
- Paint-kit instructions should identify each container, sequence or mixing, application method, compatible products/substrates, safety, cleanup, and whether decorative non-GenStone uses are supported.
- Do not advise repainting or sealing an older/faded installation until the current approved process and warranty consequences are confirmed.

## Transcript-Driven Service Clarity

The calls identify recurring service gaps but do not supersede the formal policies above. [Transcript evidence](../raw/transcripts/transcript-findings.md)

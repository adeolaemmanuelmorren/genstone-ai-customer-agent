## GenStone Visualizer

Source: [GenStone Visualizer](https://genstone.com/visualizer), verified
2026-08-08.

- Purpose: submit a photo of a home or improvement area for a custom GenStone
  rendering.
- This is the approved project-image submission page. It is **not** a support,
  damage, warranty, or claim-photo upload page.
- The form asks for email, phone, GenStone style, color, project details, and a
  photo of the improvement area.
- The page accepts JPG, PNG, and GIF images up to 15 MB.
- The agent may direct a caller to `genstone.com/visualizer` when they want to
  begin a visualizer request or need the project-photo submission page.
- Do not quote a price or turnaround time unless current approved knowledge
  explicitly supplies one.
- If the caller has trouble using the page or asks about an existing rendering,
  collect the relevant context and use the normal follow-up path.
